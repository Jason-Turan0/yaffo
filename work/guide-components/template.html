09 / SHOW THE RELEVANT INSTRUCTIONS

First run the helper. After it finishes, remove the pipx-managed package.

pipx uninstall yaffo
The helper asks whether to keep Yaffo’s user data. Your original photo and video folders are not removed by this command.
