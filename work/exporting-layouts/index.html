Keep in mind

What happens when a photo leaves the selection?

The review before enabling explains the destination’s removal policy. Removing a photo from its selected album, making it stop matching the filter, or deleting it from Yaffo can remove the copy at the destination:

DestinationRemoval behavior
FolderRemove from destination moves only unchanged copies Yaffo made to the Trash. Keep at destination leaves copies there. Other files and edited copies are protected.
OneDriveYaffo removes its own unchanged copy. A copy changed remotely is protected.
Google PhotosYaffo removes membership from its album; the uploaded photo stays in your Google Photos library.
ImmichThe photo leaves the album. A photo Yaffo uploaded also goes to Immich’s trash; a photo already there before the sync is kept.
Custom serviceThe published service defines what can be removed; check its setup review.

If a remote copy is deleted, Items can show that it was removed there. Yaffo keeps the library photo and does not automatically send that same copy again.

Each sync has one direction. Imports do not bring over albums or send library edits back to the source. Creating an import and a send sync for the same service does not make a two-way mirror.
